# Free Resources Library

Everything on this page is **free to read or watch**. Some courses (Coursera) are free to *audit* and charge only for a certificate. The list is organized by how you learn best, and each entry notes which of the four courses it supports. Every chapter also ends with its own targeted "Watch & read" list.

> [!TIP]
> A good rhythm: read the chapter here and run the code, watch one lecture on the same topic, then do the chapter's exercises. Teaching the idea back to yourself in the scratchpad, by changing an input and predicting the result, is the fastest way to make it stick.

## Free textbooks

- [read] [OpenStax: *Principles of Finance*](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-finance). Peer-reviewed, complete introductory finance textbook with practice problems. Covers FIN 101–102 and parts of 201. The best companion to this book.
- [read] [OpenStax: *Principles of Accounting, Volume 1: Financial Accounting*](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-financial-accounting). For deeper study of the financial statements in chapters 5–6.
- [read] [Python for Economics and Finance (QuantEcon)](https://python.quantecon.org/). Free online book teaching Python, NumPy and Matplotlib through economics and finance.
- [read] [Bogleheads wiki](https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Main_Page). A community-written, non-commercial encyclopedia of personal investing (Chapter 7, FIN 201).

## Data you can analyze

- [data] [FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis)](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/). Interest rates, inflation, yield spreads and 800,000+ economic series.
- [data] [U.S. Treasury interest rates](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates). Daily par yield curves and real (TIPS) yields.
- [data] [Kenneth French Data Library](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html). Market and Fama-French factor returns since 1926.
- [data] [Damodaran Online datasets](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/datacurrent.html). Industry betas, margins, multiples, cost of capital, equity risk premiums.
- [data] [SEC EDGAR full-text search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/). Every US public company's 10-K and 10-Q filings.
- [data] [Cboe VIX](https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/vix/). Implied volatility of the S&P 500.

## Ideas from the source: Nobel Prize lectures

The Nobel Prize site has the laureates' own lectures and accessible background essays on the ideas in this book.

- [read] [1985: Franco Modigliani](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/1985/summary/). Saving and capital structure (chapters 7, 13).
- [read] [1990: Markowitz, Miller and Sharpe](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/1990/summary/). Portfolio theory, capital structure and CAPM (chapters 13, 15–16).
- [read] [1997: Merton and Scholes](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/1997/summary/). Option pricing (chapters 21–22).
- [read] [2002: Daniel Kahneman](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2002/summary/). Prospect theory (Chapter 24).
- [read] [2013: Fama, Hansen and Shiller](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2013/summary/). Efficient markets and asset prices (chapters 16, 24).
- [read] [2017: Richard Thaler](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2017/summary/). Behavioral economics (Chapter 24).

## Reading for judgment, not just technique

- [read] [Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letters](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/letters.html). Warren Buffett on valuation, accounting, risk and temperament, free since 1977.
- [read] [Damodaran's blog, *Musings on Markets*](https://aswathdamodaran.blogspot.com/). Real-world valuations, step by step.
- [read] [S&P SPIVA scorecards](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/research-insights/spiva/). How active funds actually perform against benchmarks.
- [read] [Federal Reserve stress tests](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/stress-tests-capital-planning.htm). How regulators test bank resilience.

## Run this book offline

```bash
git clone https://github.com/AmolPrakash/Finance101.git
cd Finance101
pip install -e ".[dev,book]"
python book/build.py --serve
```

Then open `http://localhost:8000`. The same Python code also runs in Jupyter, VS Code or any editor. Try `python examples/01_time_value_of_money.py`.

> [!NOTE]
> Links were chosen for long-term stability (official course pages, publishers, regulators and data providers), but websites do reorganize. If a link breaks, searching for the resource's title will almost always find its new home.
